# Audio

- `public/assets/audio/crowd-cheer.mp3`: “University Indoor crowd, auditorium, graduation, applause 1” by FOSSarts, August 9, 2024. CC0 public domain dedication. The game uses the publicly available high-quality MP3 preview and plays its first eight seconds with an envelope.
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/FOSSarts/sounds/750566/
  License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Music, correct/incorrect cues, completion fanfare and confetti sounds are original procedural compositions implemented in `src/game-audio.ts`. No external music service is required.
